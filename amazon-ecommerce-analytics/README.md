# 🛒 Amazon E-Commerce Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes Amazon e-commerce product data to identify trends in product performance, pricing, discounts, ratings, and customer engagement.

The project combines **Python for exploratory data analysis** and **Power BI for interactive business intelligence and visualization**.

The goal is to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful insights that can help understand product performance, customer ratings, pricing patterns, and discount strategies.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze overall product performance
* Identify the most popular product categories
* Understand product rating distribution
* Analyze pricing and discount patterns
* Compare actual prices with discounted prices
* Identify highly reviewed products
* Understand the relationship between price, discount, and rating
* Build an interactive Power BI dashboard
* Present data-driven business insights

## 💡 Business Questions

This project focuses on answering questions such as:

1. Which categories contain the most products?
2. What is the average customer rating?
3. Which categories have the highest average discounts?
4. Which products have the highest rating counts?
5. How are product prices distributed?
6. Is there a relationship between product price and rating?
7. Does a higher discount correspond to higher ratings?
8. Which products combine high ratings with strong customer engagement?
